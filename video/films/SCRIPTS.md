# Scripts for two concept films, and how they ranked

Both films are Product Films: no presenter, silent, on-screen words only in their own moments. A script here is the
set of on-screen lines plus the order of the product beats. Each track got three candidates. Each was scored twice,
0 to 2 per point, against the two voice skills' own final checks:

- **Bob lens** (8 points): one central tension; the common assumption is told apart from what really happens; terms
  are plain; evidence is concrete and honestly framed; the framework is memorable; repetition earns its place; the
  close says what it means; nothing is falsely attributed.
- **Ina lens** (8 points): the opening sets up a journey; benefit and limitation are both clear; a concrete example
  makes the problem visible; each step has an input and an output; implementation follows the idea; a working demo is
  told apart from something ready for real use; the recap matches what was shown; nothing is falsely attributed.

Neither skill's author wrote, reviewed or approved any of this. The scores are my reading of each checklist.

## Agency track: reports on autopilot

**A1 "Nobody touched them"** (tension first)
1. Eight client reports went out this month.
2. Nobody touched them.
3. *(product: one report was held back, and says why)*
4. Autopilot is only safe if it knows when to stop.
5. *(product: the fix, then the client's page under the agency's name)*
6. Reports on autopilot. In development.

**A2 "From export to inbox"** (step by step)
1. Here is how a client report sends itself.
2. First, the data arrives on its own.
3. Then every number is checked against last month.
4. If something looks wrong, it holds.
5. If not, it sends, under your name.
6. Reports on autopilot. In development.

**A3 "14 hours"** (number first)
1. Client reporting: 14 hours a month.
2. Now: zero.
3. *(product montage)*
4. Reports on autopilot.

| Script | Bob (of 16) | Ina (of 16) | Total | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 15 | 12 | 27 | One tension (autopilot versus trust), a concrete failure on screen, and the close says what it means. Loses Ina points for no opening roadmap. |
| A2 | 10 | 15 | 25 | Clear journey with an input and output per step. Five text moments is too much reading for 35 seconds, and it has no tension. |
| A3 | 8 | 6 | 14 | The 14 hours and zero are invented figures. Fails "evidence honestly framed" on both lenses. Rejected. |

**Chosen: A1, with A2's step order for the product beats** (data in, check, hold, fix, send).

## Warehouse track: one number everyone trusts

**W1 "Two revenues"** (tension first)
1. The board pack says 4.42M.
2. The EMEA review says 4.21M.
3. *(product: the two formulas and the 904 rows between them)*
4. Same word. Two formulas.
5. *(product: one confirmed definition, traced to its source; each person sees only their own rows)*
6. One number. Traceable. Yours.
7. For teams with a warehouse. In development.

**W2 "From question to proof"** (step by step)
1. What happens when someone asks "what was revenue?"
2. We take the confirmed definition.
3. We run it in your warehouse, as that person.
4. We show the rows behind the number.
5. And where the number comes from.
6. For teams with a warehouse. In development.

**W3 "Governed AI analytics"** (category claim)
1. AI analytics your security team will approve.
2. *(product montage)*
3. Trusted. Governed. Fast.

| Script | Bob (of 16) | Ina (of 16) | Total | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | 16 | 13 | 29 | The conflict is the example, shown before any explanation, and the same number returns at the end resolved. |
| W2 | 9 | 15 | 24 | Best data flow, but opens on a question with no stake, and "we" over playing UI needs narration this film does not have. |
| W3 | 5 | 4 | 9 | "Approve" and "fast" are claims the product cannot support today; no example. Rejected. |

**Chosen: W1, with W2's order for the proof beats** (definition, run as the person, rows, trace).

## Truth notes for both

- Every screen in both films is a planned screen with sample data. None of it is available. Each film carries a
  "Concept preview" tag for its whole length and closes on "In development".
- No time-saved or adoption figures appear, because none have been measured.
- The state after a click (a held report sending, two reports agreeing) is proposed behaviour, staged for the film.
- No narration and no music, so there is nothing to license.
